Geometry Guide: Rub el Hizb (8-Fold) vs. Star of David (6-Fold)

Historically, Islamic geometric systems avoid standard hexagrams (six-pointed "Star of David" shapes) to emphasize distinct **eight-fold, ten-fold, or twelve-pointed visual motifs** (known as *Girihes* or *Khatims*).

This engine guarantees accurate visual ratios utilizing nested square overlays ($45^\circ$ rotations) and golden ratios ($1:\sqrt{2}$), providing an unmistakable classic Andalusia or Ottoman Mosque aesthetic.
